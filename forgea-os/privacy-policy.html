Privacy Policy

How ForgeaOS and Reva Infotech collect, use, and protect your information.

Last updated August 26, 2026

Overview

This policy explains how Reva Infotech LLP ("Reva Infotech," "we," "us") handles information in connection with the ForgeaOS website (forgeaos.com) and the ForgeaOS platform (app.forgeaos.com). ForgeaOS is a product built and operated by Reva Infotech.

We wrote this policy to describe what we actually do, in plain language. If you have questions after reading it, email us at info@reva-infotech.com.

Information we collect

Information you give us directly. When you email us, book a demo, or start a trial, you share information such as your name, email address, company, and whatever else you choose to include in your message.

Information from using the website. This marketing website does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and it does not set tracking cookies. The only thing it stores in your browser is a single local-storage entry that remembers whether you prefer light or dark mode — that preference stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Information from the ForgeaOS platform. If your organization uses ForgeaOS, we process the account information and operational data (for example, procurement, inventory, maintenance, or workforce records) that you and your team enter, solely to provide the platform to your organization.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the ForgeaOS website and platform.
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule demos, and provide support.
  • To send service-related communications, such as replies to your messages or updates about a trial or account.
  • To meet legal, security, and contractual obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Third-party services

A few parts of this site hand you off to services we don't operate, each governed by its own privacy practices:

  • Calendly — used for booking demo calls. See Calendly's privacy policy.
  • Your email provider — "email us" links open your own mail client; the message is sent directly to us.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers — used to run the ForgeaOS platform and store account data on our behalf, under our instructions.

Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described above — for example, for as long as your organization has an active ForgeaOS account, or until an inquiry is resolved — unless a longer period is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, email info@reva-infotech.com and we'll respond as required by applicable law.

Data security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

ForgeaOS is a business product. It is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from, children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or platform changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
