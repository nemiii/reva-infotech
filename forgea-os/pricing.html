Good to know

Pricing questions, answered.

Is the price the same in India and outside India?
Yes. Every plan and module is published in USD at one worldwide price. Indian customers may pay the INR equivalent at checkout — that's a payment conversion, not a separate price list. Local taxes such as GST or VAT are collected in addition to the listed price.
Is there a free trial, and do I need a credit card?
Yes. Eligible modules include a 14-day self-service trial, and no credit card is required to start.
Can I pay annually?
Yes. Annual billing is available on every plan and standalone module: pay for 10 months and get 12 months of access — roughly two months free.
What happens if I exceed my document storage allowance?
You'll get usage notices at 80%, 90%, and 100% of your allowance. At the limit, existing files stay exactly where they are — only new uploads pause until you free up space or add a storage add-on. We never delete business documents automatically.
How are drivers billed?
Named mobile-driver users are billed separately from full-access seats, at a lower rate, because GPS telemetry, sync, storage, and support carry their own ongoing cost. Drivers are never billed — or free — as full-access users when GPS tracking is active.
Will my price change after I subscribe?
Launch prices are protected for the first 12 months of your paid subscription. Any future price change applies only to new subscriptions, unless your contract explicitly permits renewal changes.
